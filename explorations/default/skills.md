A general-purpose daily news monitor covering world affairs, technology, business, science, and society — turning hundreds of web articles into a concise intelligence brief using a local LLM. No cloud, no subscriptions.

## Research Domains

- **Geopolitics & World Affairs** — Conflicts, diplomacy, elections, sanctions, international summits
- **Technology & Innovation** — Breakthroughs, major product launches, startups, semiconductors, space
- **Business & Economy** — Markets, central banks, earnings, M&A, commodities, energy
- **Science & Environment** — Climate policy, scientific discoveries, disasters, renewables, health
- **Society & Policy** — Social movements, education, healthcare, immigration, human rights

## What It Produces

Each run searches hundreds of articles across these domains, removes duplicates from previous reports, and synthesises a structured Markdown brief with an Executive Summary followed by numbered per-source, per-date findings in each domain.

## Customising

Open ⚙️ Settings → Topics to add, remove, or rewrite research queries. Open ⚙️ Settings → Schedule to change when the brief runs.
