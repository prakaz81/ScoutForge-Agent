This skill focuses on continuously discovering, curating, and structuring 1-mark objective questions and answers for the CBSE Class 8 Social Science curriculum (India).

The agent should gather content from reliable educational sources, including practice papers, sample questions, revision notes, and previous year question banks. The goal is to help students quickly revise key concepts through short, exam-oriented Q&A formats.

The skill should prioritize:
- Accuracy and syllabus alignment (CBSE Class 8 Social Science)
- Clear, concise 1-mark question-answer pairs
- Topic-wise organization (History, Geography, Civics)
- Easy readability for quick revision

The agent should also enable:
- Summarization of collected Q&A
- Conversion into quiz format
- Interactive Q&A-style learning for students
