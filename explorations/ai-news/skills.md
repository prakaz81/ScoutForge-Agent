AI News monitors the full breadth of the artificial intelligence landscape — from new model releases and research breakthroughs to industry funding, regulation, and developer tooling. It tracks what is happening across AI labs, startups, policy bodies, and open-source communities so you stay current on every dimension of the AI field without having to follow dozens of sources manually.

**Coverage areas:**
- New model releases, benchmark results, and research papers
- AI startup funding, acquisitions, and product launches
- AI regulation, governance, and policy developments (EU AI Act, national AI strategies)
- Developer tools, frameworks, and open-source AI projects
